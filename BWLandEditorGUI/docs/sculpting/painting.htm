Painting countries

Country Paint changes the country index of cells without changing their elevation.

Brush

Country brushes support saved presets, Circle/Triangle shape, size, W/H proportions and angle/camera rotation. Select the target country directly or pick it from the map.

Filters

Only cells matching all enabled conditions are painted:

  • Surface class: over land, over coastline and/or near coastline.
  • Slope range: minimum and maximum slope.
  • Height range: minimum and maximum elevation.

An optional environment sound can be written together with the country. This is useful when a biome change should also change ambience.
