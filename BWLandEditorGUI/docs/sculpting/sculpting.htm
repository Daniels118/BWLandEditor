Sculpting the landscape

The Sculpt tool applies a reusable brush to terrain elevations and can also assign countries, coastline/cliff materials and sounds while shaping.

Brush controls

Brushes can be saved, added and removed. Shapes are Circle and Triangle. Size defines the major dimension; W/H change the aspect ratio. The angle can follow the camera or use a fixed offset. Flow controls editing speed and Smoothness controls the feathered edge.

While a brush is active, Alt + right-drag changes size and angle directly in the viewport. Left-drag applies the brush. Shift acts as an inverse/alternate modifier for sculpt modes.

Sculpt modes

ModeBehaviorShift behavior
AbsoluteMoves cells toward the configured absolute elevation.Drives cells away from the reference elevation toward the available extremes.
RelativeAdds the configured elevation delta to the height present at stroke start.Subtracts the delta.
FlattenUses the elevation under the stroke start point as a flat target.Moves cells back toward the heights recorded at stroke start.
SmoothMoves each cell toward a weighted average of neighboring heights.Produces the inverse effect (local sharpening).
TerraceQuantizes terrain into elevation steps using the configured elevation as the terrace interval.The terrace target remains quantized; the modifier still affects directional water/coast handling.

Terrain constraints and automatic painting

  • Max slope limits the allowed height difference to neighboring cells for most sculpt operations.
  • Max depth limits how deep the brush can cut into water levels.
  • Normal / cliff / coastline countries can be assigned while sculpting. Cliff classification uses the configured cliff slope.
  • An optional sound can be painted on ordinary land while water-related cells receive appropriate automatic water/coast sounds.
  • Changed blocks have heights fitted to LND storage precision and shadows are recomputed at the end of the stroke.
